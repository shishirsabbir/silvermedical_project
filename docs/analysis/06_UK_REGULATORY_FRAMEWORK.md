# UK regulatory framework

Baseline: 7 November 2026. Operational planning guide; product-specific release requires documented classification and review of the actual supply arrangement. Never use a generic website badge as proof of authorisation.

## Jurisdiction and activity first

Record destination as Great Britain (England, Scotland, Wales), Northern Ireland or another country. Then determine product regime, intended user, manufacturer and our role. A UK warehouse, UK company or EU supplier alone does not establish permission to sell.

## Human medical devices and IVDs in Great Britain

The UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002, as amended, govern the GB route. Published MHRA guidance retains conditional CE recognition: eligible MDD/AIMDD devices generally to certificate expiry or 30 June 2028, eligible IVDD devices to certificate expiry or 30 June 2030, and MDR/IVDR devices to 30 June 2030. Exceptions, self-declared products and transition eligibility must be checked by SKU. Proposed indefinite recognition must not be treated as enacted permission. [MHRA GB guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/regulating-medical-devices-in-the-uk).

MHRA registration of devices is separate from conformity assessment. Manufacturers outside the UK need the applicable UK Responsible Person arrangement. A GB importer must notify the manufacturer/UKRP, which supplies importer details to MHRA. A distributor that does not import devices for placement on the GB market is not required to register as that distributor. Registration is not MHRA approval or endorsement. [MHRA registration guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/register-medical-devices-to-place-on-the-market).

For each device, retain the applicable declaration of conformity, certificate where required, exact manufacturer/model identifiers, label and instructions, intended purpose, regulatory route, UKRP/importer evidence where applicable, certificate validity and traceability details. Do not demand a notified-body certificate for every self-declared device or treat an ISO certificate as a substitute for product conformity evidence.

GB post-market surveillance rules took effect on 16 June 2025. They impose manufacturer obligations; the distributor's process should capture complaints, preserve lots/serials, escalate to the responsible operator and cooperate with corrective actions. Taking on manufacturer responsibilities changes the obligation set. [MHRA PMS scope](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/medical-devices-post-market-surveillance-requirements/introduction-and-scope).

## Northern Ireland

NI follows EU MDR/IVDR device arrangements. CE and any applicable UKNI route must be checked separately; a CE+UKNI product does not thereby gain EU-market access. MHRA guidance describes mandatory use of four EUDAMED modules from 28 May 2026, with role/device-dependent requirements and transition details. Do not require every ordinary distributor to obtain an actor registration merely because importers must do so. [NI device guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/medical-devices-eu-regulations-for-mdr-and-ivdr).

Make NI an explicit checkout permission. An item approved for GB must pass the NI dossier before a delivery address can be accepted.

## PPE

Establish intended wearer protection and whether the item is also a medical device. A respirator, surgical mask and general face covering are not interchangeable regulatory labels. Review the relevant PPE declaration, conformity route, marking, instructions, importer/distributor evidence and protective claims. Dual-purpose products may need both regimes. Applicable current standards must be taken from the manufacturer's evidence and current designated/harmonised lists rather than guessed. [OPSS PPE guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/personal-protective-equipment-enforcement-regulations-2018).

## Disinfectants and chemicals

Device disinfectants, general surface disinfectants and skin products can follow different routes. The MHRA notes that surgical scrubs and certain antimicrobial pre-injection products are medicines, whereas some other cleaning products are biocides or devices. [Borderline guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/borderlines-with-medical-devices/borderlines-with-medical-devices-and-other-products-in-great-britain).

For biocides, establish the applicable GB or NI authorisation/transitional route, permitted use, supplier eligibility, label and chemical documentation. Do not assume every biocide needs an identical authorisation today or that EU permission covers GB. [HSE introduction](https://www.hse.gov.uk/biocides/about/introduction.htm), [HSE authorisation](https://www.hse.gov.uk/biocides/products/product-authorisation-overview.htm).

Obtain safety data and determine transport classification, packaging and carrier acceptance. Hazardous goods cannot be assigned a generic standard-parcel promise. [Moving dangerous goods](https://www.gov.uk/moving-dangerous-goods).

## Human medicines

Create a separate medicines business case. Supply to a patient, wholesale supply to a clinic, imports and brokering are different activities. MHRA guidance identifies wholesale-distribution licensing for supply other than to the patient, with activity-specific rules and exceptions requiring assessment. EU purchasing does not remove the need to establish a lawful medicines import route, approved premises, responsible personnel and GDP controls. [MHRA licensing](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-manufacturer-or-wholesaler-of-medicines-licences).

Separate GSL, pharmacy and prescription-only routes and verify the actual marketing authorisation and permitted territory. Pharmacy/POM patient supply requires the appropriate pharmacy/professional model. The EU distance-selling logo stopped being required for GB-based online medicine sellers from 1 January 2021, while NI has its own scheme; do not add a copied logo as a generic trust badge. [MHRA online-sale guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/register-for-the-distance-selling-logo), [MHRA seller register](https://medicine-seller-register.mhra.gov.uk/).

Budget premises, professional staffing, GDP procedures, customer entitlement checks, storage mapping, temperature monitoring, recalls and inspections. Medicines drop-shipping or directing supply can still be regulated; a licensed partner's authorisation does not automatically cover our legal activities. Controlled drugs need a separate Home Office analysis, including exemptions and NI differences. [Controlled-drug domestic licences](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/controlled-drugs-domestic-licences).

Public advertising of prescription medicines and professional promotion require distinct treatment. Keep medicine content out of automatic general-product campaigns until reviewed. [MHRA advertising guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/advertise-your-medicines).

## Veterinary medicines and products

Do not treat every veterinary instrument as a human medical device. Veterinary medicine supply follows its own framework. Current VMD guidance requires online registration for POM-V, POM-VPS and NFA-VPS routes, while AVM-GSL and Schedule 6 products have exceptions. Appropriate UK premises, professional entitlement, authorisation and supplied logos matter. [VMD internet sales](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sell-veterinary-medicines-on-the-internet).

The VMD retail guidance also identifies special GB-to-NI restrictions. Review the complete lane before offering UK-wide veterinary medicines. [VMD retail supply](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/retail-of-veterinary-medicines).

## Specialist cases

| Product/activity | Required investigation |
|---|---|
| Professional IVD versus home self-test | Confirm manufacturer's intended user/use; do not relabel a professional test for home use |
| Sharps/blades | Review actual product against age-restriction and remote-delivery rules; not all sharps are legally identical |
| Medical oxygen/gases | Medicine authorisation, cylinder/transport rules, storage and trained carrier |
| Radiation equipment | Supply, installation, maintenance and employer notifications/registration/consents for actual activity |
| Software/connected equipment | Intended purpose, device status, cybersecurity, updates and data roles |
| Used/refurbished devices | Condition, intended use, decontamination, servicing, evidence and resulting operator role |
| Clinical packs/private label | Manufacturer/pack-producer and repackaging implications |
| Supplements/cosmetics/ordinary furniture | Separate applicable rules; medical marketing cannot be improvised |

References for specialist review: [Offensive Weapons Act guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-offensive-weapons-act-2019/statutory-guidance-offensive-weapons-act-2019-accessible-version), [HSE radiation overview](https://www.hse.gov.uk/radiation/ionising/notifiy-register-consent.htm). These are starting points, not completed product-specific opinions. Recheck reforms and commencement dates before launch.
