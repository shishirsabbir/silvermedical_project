Baseline: 7 November 2026. Channels and targets are proposals pending buyer, product and economics validation.

## Positioning and channels

Test a clear offer around reliable professional procurement: correct specification, transparent delivered cost, dependable replenishment, shelf-life information and competent support. Avoid unsupported claims of NHS approval, manufacturer authorisation, clinical superiority or lowest prices.

| Channel | Proposed use | Prerequisite | Evaluate with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic search | Product identifiers, category and specialty demand | Original accurate product data and indexable approved pages | Qualified traffic, search-to-order and cohort contribution |
| Paid search/shopping | High-intent approved products | Policy/claim eligibility, stock, feed accuracy and margin | CM2 and payback, not only ROAS |
| Direct B2B sales | Organisations and multi-site accounts | Lawful contact approach, relevant basket/quote and credit policy | Qualified opportunities, contribution and DSO |
| Manufacturer partnerships | Credible technical education and referrals | Written channel/content permissions | Activated accounts and recurring contribution |
| Email/replenishment | Repeat purchases and useful alerts | Valid messaging basis, accurate stock and opt-out | Incremental retained contribution |
| WhatsApp/SMS | Evaluate eligible messaging use cases | Channel-specific feasibility and permissions; see analysis/21 before committing WhatsApp | Incremental contribution, delivery and opt-out rates |
| CRM/support | Connect enquiries, orders and resolution history | Staff ownership, data access and escalation | Resolution time, repeat contacts and retained customers |
| Tenders | Contracted institutional demand | Qualification evidence and bid resources | Risk-adjusted bid value and contract margin |

Do not spend broadly on every category before product approval and fulfilment readiness. Full catalogue coverage can be supported by separate acquisition budgets and eligibility rules.

## Privacy and direct marketing

ICO guidance distinguishes corporate subscribers from sole traders and some partnerships for electronic marketing. Named business contacts still involve personal-data obligations. Public availability of an address is not consent. Maintain suppression lists, identify the sender and provide a working opt-out. Apply the correct consent or soft-opt-in analysis before using individual-subscriber contacts. [ICO B2B marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing), [ICO electronic-mail guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-electronic-mail-marketing-rules/).

For calls, assess TPS/CTPS and applicable calling rules. Do not turn a downloaded healthcare directory into an assumed permission-to-market list. Separate transactional order messages from marketing and assess back-in-stock/reorder messages by their content and purpose.

The ICO finalised storage/access technology guidance in April 2026, including newer exceptions and conditions. Classify each cookie, pixel and SDK by purpose. Default to blocking advertising tracking until valid consent; assess any statistical-purpose exception against all current conditions before relying on it. Old blanket cookie summaries may be outdated. [Current ICO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/).

Minimise personal data, set retention periods, document lawful bases and processors, handle rights requests, secure accounts and assess international transfers. Avoid patient data and health-condition-based ad audiences in ordinary ecommerce workflows. A tax-relief declaration may require more sensitive handling than a routine order address.

## Claims and reputation

Use the manufacturer's substantiated intended-purpose language. Escalate disease-treatment, diagnostic accuracy, pathogen-kill, sterile, protective and medicine claims. Public prescription-medicine advertising has separate restrictions. [MHRA medicines advertising](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/advertise-your-medicines).

Publish authentic reviews with a moderation and verification policy; do not manufacture reviews, conceal incentives or selectively present ratings in a misleading way. Track and resolve causes of complaints rather than using reviews purely as acquisition assets. [CMA unfair commercial practices](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/unfair-commercial-practices-cma207).

## Retention without margin leakage

Prioritise saved baskets, organisation lists, stock continuity, minimum shelf life, compatible consumables and useful order reminders. Cost loyalty discounts by category and cohort. A universal percentage discount can destroy the margin on price-sensitive branded equipment.

Subscriptions require a separate commercial/legal design; do not enable automatic recurring orders merely because consumables repeat. Validate buyer approval procedures, changing clinical needs and shelf-life consumption first.

## Measurement framework

Record product view, identifier search, zero result, filter use, add-to-basket, restriction block, quote request, checkout, approved order, actual dispatch, delivery, return, refund and reorder. Deduplicate order conversions and reconcile analytics revenue with finance, net of cancellations and refunds. Honour tracking permissions.

Weekly measures: approval-ready assortment, availability, search success, basket conversion, AOV excluding VAT, CM1, CM2, CAC, delivery accuracy, support resolution, refund age and cash. Monthly: cohort repeat contribution, retention, stock ageing, expiry, supplier concentration, DSO and cash runway. Segment by category, buyer, channel, fulfilment route and destination.

No keyword volumes, CAC, conversion rates or retention benchmarks have been measured for the new brand. Use pilot evidence before setting a growth forecast.
