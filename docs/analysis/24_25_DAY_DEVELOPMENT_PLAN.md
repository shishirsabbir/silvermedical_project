## New decisions versus assumptions

Confirmed direction: owners participate in the first-day design process, choose visual/functionality directions, and receive a first-selling feature set for their reported warehouse range. No actual 250-item stock file or product evidence was supplied. The 250-SKU interpretation is a planning assumption; variants/units need reconciliation.

Proposed workshop: three concepts, a side-by-side choice, one selected direction, a screen/feature review and refinement by Day 3. Proposed assets include logo variants, a small brand guide, responsive website concepts, three banners with desktop/mobile exports, category/social/email templates and outer shipping-packaging concepts. These quantities are not an accepted asset-production contract. Original medical packaging/labels and factual product appearance are preserved.

The Day 0 calendar remains illustrative: 15 November 2026 preparation; Day 1 workshop on 16 November; Day 25 release target on 10 December. All are calendar days, with no working arrangement booked. Later phase targets remain unchanged.

## Delivery controls

The first ten representative records validate the data model; they are part of batch 1. Process five batches of 50 by Days 7/9/11/13/15. Day 18 accounts for all 250 as approved + held + excluded/duplicate. Only approved eligible records with sellable stock go live. Record any reduced assortment honestly and obtain the owner's explicit release decision; do not treat missing product permissions as an engineering exception.

Day 5 proves one full transaction before wider development. Day 15 rechecks capacity, approvals and remaining scope. Days 19-24 cover critical tests, business acceptance, correction, recovery rehearsal and release decision. Software, catalogue preparation and provider/operator readiness are parallel workstreams.

AI roles help with creative alternatives, authorised data cleanup, UI/code and review. They do not establish product facts, licensing, trademark clearance or human delivery capacity. No agents were spawned and no branded assets or application were generated through this planning task.
