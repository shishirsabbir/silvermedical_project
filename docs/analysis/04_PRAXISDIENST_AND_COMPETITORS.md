# Praxisdienst and competitors

Observed: 7 November 2026. Features and prices are published observations, not independent service tests. A professional-only benchmark's terms cannot simply become a mixed consumer store's terms.

## Praxisdienst operating patterns

The homepage exposes human, dental and veterinary divisions; detailed product categories; search by identifiers; lists; account-based repeat orders; comparison tools; and product availability states. The useful principle is to help a professional buy an accurate recurring basket quickly. [Praxisdienst homepage](https://www.praxisdienst.com/).

Its FAQ states that offers target professional/institutional use, with net prices and destination-dependent tax/shipping. This is a benchmark for professional procurement, not a conclusion about UK consumer price presentation. [FAQ](https://www.praxisdienst.com/en-en/information-pages/contact/frequently-asked-questions-faqs).

Its PD PLUS page describes a 5% discount programme, a one-year term and no automatic renewal; the page text lists EUR75. Do not turn that into a verified UK membership quote without checking destination and checkout. [PD PLUS](https://www.praxisdienst.com/product/praxisdienst-plus/pdplus).

For selected products, the guaranteed-usable-until date is visible before purchase. This is particularly relevant for buyers balancing order size against expiry risk. [Shelf-life feature](https://www.praxisdienst.com/en-en/guaranteed-usable-until-date/).

| Observed pattern | Proposed application | Dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Deep taxonomy | Product and specialty navigation | Clean PIM data and agreed category tree |
| Identifier search | Exact MPN/GTIN plus synonyms and units | Reliable supplier data |
| Repeat ordering | Reorder past basket with changes shown | Stock, price and restriction revalidation |
| Lists | Lists by clinic, procedure or location | Organisation accounts |
| Availability states | Separate own stock, supplier-direct and backorder | Fresh availability feeds and promise engine |
| Expiry visibility | Guaranteed minimum remaining shelf life | Lot-aware inventory and allocation |
| Loyalty | Costed benefit for repeat buyers | Positive basket contribution after discounts |
| Professional advice | Technical support with escalation | Trained team and manufacturer support |

These are functional requirements to evaluate, not a request to reproduce proprietary text, branding or assets.

## UK delivery benchmark

| Supplier | Published offer observed | Caveat | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medisave | Free delivery above GBP35 excluding VAT; free service within five working days; paid next-working-day option with 4pm cutoff | Geographic, stock and manufacturer-direct exceptions; another policy page shows a different cutoff | [Shipping and returns](https://www.medisave.co.uk/pages/shipping-and-returns) |
| MediSupplies | Free next-working-day mainland delivery from GBP50 excluding VAT/carriage; 4:30pm in-stock cutoff | Off-mainland charges and category exceptions; some products ship separately | [Carriage](https://www.medisupplies.co.uk/page/Carriage) |
| EconoMed | GBP4.95 below GBP85; free from GBP85; 1–3-business-day total estimate | Company-published claims, not verified delivery performance | [Shipping policy](https://www.economed.co.uk/policies/shipping-policy) |

A shipping threshold is not comparable until VAT basis, postcode, package size, service speed, stock and extra charges match. Do not choose a threshold by copying the lowest competitor.

## Competitive position to test

Test dependable documentation, shelf-life certainty and fewer procurement errors alongside basket price. Established sellers can compete on brand access, service, breadth and credit; winning through headline discount alone may leave insufficient contribution.

No claim is made that the new store will be cheapest. Complete the [matched basket](../templates/COMPETITOR_BASKET.md) with manufacturer identifiers, pack sizes, stock, gross and net totals, freight and dates before drawing a price ranking. Additional specialist supplier comparisons should follow category selection; coverage beyond the three UK examples above is not complete.
