## Map the actual supply lane

| Lane | Commercial attractions | Questions before use |
|---|---|---|
| UK authorised distributor → our GB stock | Domestic replenishment and potentially simpler paperwork | Genuine supply chain, reseller rights, actual importer/UKRP upstream |
| EU manufacturer/distributor → our GB stock | Broader range and possible better purchasing terms | GB importer role, device/medicine admissibility, customs, origin, FX and freight |
| UK supplier → UK buyer directly | Less inventory exposure for large equipment | Who sells/imports, availability feed, fulfilment SLA, invoice/returns and warranty |
| EU supplier → UK buyer directly | Access to long-tail equipment | Importer and duties, product eligibility, DDP feasibility, customer consent and lead time |
| EU supplier → EU warehouse → EU buyer | Candidate later European model | EU importer/economic-operator allocation, local VAT, country-language requirements |

Supplier country, dispatch country, manufacturer country and customs origin are separate data fields. An EU invoice does not establish EU origin or GB device-market eligibility.

## Supplier qualification

Verify legal identity, relevant licences/authorisations, manufacturer relationship, physical dispatch location and bank details independently. Obtain exact product identifiers, authenticity/traceability evidence, documentation and territorial online resale rights. Confirm that image, IFU and product-data licences permit our use and translation where appropriate.

Agree minimum order, case quantities, price breaks, currency, rebate conditions, payment terms, lead time, fill rate, stock-feed frequency, minimum shelf life, substitution rules, allocation during shortages, recalls, returns and manufacturer warranty coverage. Record whether a certificate covers the actual SKU/variant rather than a similarly named family.

Compare continuity and claims support alongside purchase price. Have alternative supply for critical fast movers, but do not silently substitute a medically different product. The [supplier form](../templates/SUPPLIER_QUALIFICATION.md) is the intake record.

## Import and tax workflow

For GB imports, establish the GB EORI, declarant/agent, commodity code, customs valuation, origin, import VAT, any duty and product-specific permissions. Keep customs entries reconciled to receipts and supplier invoices. [HMRC import steps](https://www.gov.uk/import-goods-into-uk).

UK–EU tariff preference depends on qualifying origin and evidence, not simply shipping from the EU. Re-exporting unchanged products through GB can create additional duty and paperwork. Test returned-goods or other relief only with evidence and customs advice. [UK–EU origin rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/general-rules-to-determine-the-origin-your-products-for-trade-between-the-uk-and-eu).

For an eligible VAT-registered operator, postponed import VAT accounting can change cash timing; recovery remains subject to the normal entitlement and documentation. It does not remove customs duty or make product costs zero. [HMRC import VAT returns](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/complete-your-vat-return-to-account-for-import-vat).

## Landed-cost record

    Landed cost per sellable unit = (purchase cost + allocated inbound freight + duty + brokerage + insurance + nonrecoverable tax + inbound handling + expected loss) / sellable units received

Convert foreign currency at the actual hedged/settlement assumption and model a separate downside FX case. Recoverable VAT is normally a working-capital item, not a permanent product cost. Separate damaged/short-dated writeoffs from reusable saleable units.

Use contracted trade terms and named places. DDP/DAP or another Incoterm allocates costs/tasks but does not by itself settle every medical-device or medicines regulatory responsibility. Get written role allocation per lane.

## Contract and supplier-direct controls

Require evidence updates when certificates, ingredients, labels, manufacturing locations or authorisations change. Define complaints/recall notification time, audit access, indemnities, insurance and termination/stock runout. Require data accuracy and order acceptance/rejection feeds. Avoid unconditional delivery promises based on an old supplier feed.

For direct shipping, agree tracking, split shipment costs, neutral packaging, customer data processing, delivery inspection, failed delivery, returns address, serial/lot reporting and support ownership. Do not publish supplier availability as our warehouse stock.

Score qualified suppliers on total cost, range, evidence quality, service, shelf life and concentration risk. Stop approval if authenticity, lawful supply or documentation cannot be established, even if the commercial score is attractive.
