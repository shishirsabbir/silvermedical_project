# Catalogue and category gates

Baseline: 7 November 2026. All product families remain within the planning scope. The classifications below are routing prompts: intended purpose, claims, design, regulatory evidence and destination decide the actual route for each SKU.

## Proposed full catalogue taxonomy

| Family | Example subcategories | Approval questions before activation |
|---|---|---|
| Examination consumables | Couch rolls, applicators, tongue depressors, covers | Medical intended use? Pack/sterility/expiry? |
| Dressings and wound care | Gauze, adhesive dressings, bandages, advanced dressings | Device class, ingredients, sterile status and intended wound use |
| Injection and infusion | Needles, syringes, cannulas, tubing, tourniquets | Device evidence, compatible connectors, professional/consumer suitability |
| Blood collection | Tubes, holders, lancets, accessories | Device/IVD accessory route, additive and equipment compatibility |
| PPE and protective clothing | Gloves, masks, respirators, gowns, eye protection | PPE/device/dual route, intended protection and declarations |
| Infection control | Device disinfectants, surface products, hand products, dispensers | Device, biocide, medicine or cosmetic boundary; chemical shipping |
| General diagnostics | Stethoscopes, BP monitors, thermometers, otoscopes, scales | Intended use, conformity, calibration, accessories and power |
| Specialist diagnostics | ECG, spirometry, ultrasound, dermatoscopy, audiology | Installation, software, service, training and conformity |
| IVD and point-of-care testing | Reagents, analyzers, test strips, self-tests | IVD class/route, professional versus self-test use, storage and lot trace |
| Emergency and resuscitation | AEDs, pads, batteries, airways, first-aid kits | Compatibility, expiry, intended users and medical/medicine contents |
| Surgical and minor-procedure supplies | Instruments, blades, sutures, packs | Sterile/reusable status, blade-sale rules, packs and reprocessing |
| Sterilisation and decontamination | Autoclaves, cleaners, indicators, pouches | Device/accessory route, commissioning, validation and servicing |
| Medical furniture | Couches, stools, trolleys, storage, lighting | Ordinary furniture or device? Load, fire/electrical requirements and installation |
| Patient handling and mobility | Hoists, slings, wheelchairs, mobility aids, beds | Compatibility, fitting, training, service and VAT eligibility |
| Continence and personal care | Pads, wash products, catheters, bags | Intended purpose, invasive product route and potential VAT relief |
| Laboratory and education | Tubes, pipettes, centrifuges, microscopes, models | Clinical/IVD versus research/education use; electrical/chemical controls |
| Dental | Burs, impression materials, instruments, sterilisation, equipment | Dental-specific compatibility, chemical/device routes and service |
| Veterinary | Instruments, diagnostics, consumables, equipment | Species/intended use; do not automatically apply human-device rules |
| Medical workwear | Scrubs, coats, footwear, textiles | Ordinary workwear versus protective claims; sizing and returns |
| Facility and administrative goods | Paper, bins, labels, filing, cleaning equipment | General product rules, freight and waste obligations |
| Human medicines | GSL, pharmacy medicines, prescription medicines, vaccines, medical gases | Separate retail/wholesale/import authorisations and personnel |
| Veterinary medicines | AVM-GSL, NFA-VPS, POM-VPS, POM-V | VMD route, premises, personnel, online registration where required |
| Specialist and advanced products | Implants, radiation equipment, software, connected devices, cold-chain products | Dedicated technical, clinical/regulatory, transport and service workstreams |

The map is deliberately broad; it does not assert every listed example is suitable for the same customer or online sale route. Product claims can change regulatory classification, including for disinfectants and products with medicinal ingredients. [MHRA borderline guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/borderlines-with-medical-devices/borderlines-with-medical-devices-and-other-products-in-great-britain).

## SKU activation states

Use Draft → Evidence requested → Compliance reviewed → Commercially approved → Fulfilment validated → Active. Separate states: Restricted account, Quote only, Temporarily unavailable, Quarantined and Discontinued. Approval must be scoped to destination and sales channel. An active GB item is not automatically active in NI or the EU.

No saleable item should bypass: supplier approval, role assignment, applicable evidence, allowed claims, customer eligibility, landed margin, tax treatment, storage/shipping capability, returns/warranty route and traceability. Use the [SKU form](../templates/SKU_APPROVAL.md).

## Range economics

Score candidates on evidenced demand, repeat potential, contribution after freight, supply continuity, shelf life, inventory exposure, service burden and documentation readiness. Treat missing regulatory evidence as a gate, not a score that high margin can offset.

Commercial assortment roles can include high-comparison branded items, profitable basket additions, replenishment staples and quotation-led equipment. Keep sizes and colours as explicit variants; distinguish a box of 100 from a case containing ten boxes.

## Bundles and private label

A virtual convenience list is different from a physically assembled clinical procedure pack. Do not alter sterile packaging, decant disinfectants, translate labels, assemble clinical packs or sell devices under the new brand without reviewing the resulting role and obligations. EU pack-producer and economic-operator roles are described by the [European Commission](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-topics-interest/economic-operators_en). Detailed UK/EU activity review is required before implementation.

## Proposed activation sequence

Document-ready ordinary goods and resold products can progress alongside more complex workstreams. Medicines, cold-chain products, complex IVDs, implants and installation-heavy equipment need dedicated readiness plans. This sequence is proposed only; the user has not authorised excluding them permanently or fixed the first release assortment.
