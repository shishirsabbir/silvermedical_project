# Consumer and commercial terms

Baseline: 7 November 2026. Drafting requirements, not ready-to-publish legal terms.

## Buyer status and contractual identity

Identify the actual seller, registered business details, contact channels, supply territory and contract-acceptance point. Distinguish genuine business-purpose purchases from consumers. A professional email address, account name or blanket B2B checkbox cannot safely settle every legal buyer-status question.

If consumer purchases are enabled, consumer price and cancellation flows must work even when the catalogue is predominantly professional. Keep restricted-product eligibility separate from whether the contract is B2B or B2C.

## Consumer cancellation and defects

For eligible distance-sale goods, provide the cancellation information, process and form; customers generally have 14 days after receipt to notify cancellation and another 14 days to return. Applicable refund timing, standard delivery refund and lawful deductions need implementation. Genuine sealed health/hygiene exceptions concern the relevant goods once unsealed; they are not a blanket exclusion for all medical products. Faulty-product rights remain distinct from change of mind. [GOV.UK refunds guidance](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds).

The consumer short-term right to reject faulty goods and delivery-risk rules need clause-level checking against the Consumer Rights Act before final drafting. The primary statute pages were not reliably retrievable in this research; do not treat the prior EconoMed report or competitor wording as legal validation. [Consumer Rights Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/contents).

Do not publish a universal 48-hour defect cutoff, all-sale-items exclusion, mandatory original packaging condition for every statutory claim, or a clause transferring every consumer transit risk at courier handover. Commercial inspection requests must not erase statutory rights.

Provide order confirmation in a durable medium, delivery information and the agreed fulfilment timeframe. GOV.UK describes delivery within 30 days unless otherwise agreed. Use a genuine agreement for longer-lead equipment rather than a surprise delay after payment. [Distance selling](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses).

## Commercial terms

Define account approval, authorised purchasers, quotes, quote validity, contract prices, VAT, credit limits, PO acceptance, invoicing, payment dates, partial shipments, backorders, cancellations and commercial return allowances. B2B terms still require appropriate legal drafting; business status does not make every exclusion enforceable.

For installed equipment, define site-readiness responsibilities, delivery access, installation/acceptance, training, warranty, service scope and excluded consumables. For supplier-direct orders, the customer must still know who is responsible for problems.

## Pricing and promotions

Make unavoidable charges and tax transparent early enough for an informed decision, calculate variable charges when the address/order is known and show a complete total before purchase. Review reference-price promotions, urgency/stock claims, unit prices and conditional discounts. [CMA price guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/price-transparency-cma209/providing-clear-and-accurate-information-about-prices-summary).

## Trade credit and payments

Do not copy instant-credit offers without underwriting. Define entity checks, public-body PO validation, credit decision/limit, authorised buyer roles, fraud checks, overdue reminders, account suspension and bad-debt provision. Treat a purchase order as a procurement document, not a guarantee of payment or automatic acceptance.

Agree payment-provider acceptance for the actual catalogue, especially medicines and restricted goods, before committing to a platform or launch date. Compare gross-transaction fees, settlement timing, reserve terms, refund-fee treatment, chargeback handling and multi-currency costs using actual quotes.

Consumer credit, financed equipment and instalment products need a separate FCA/perimeter assessment if offered; this baseline has not determined authorisation or exemption requirements for a specific finance product.

## Required documents and journeys

Prepare consumer terms, commercial terms, delivery policy, cancellation/returns policy, warranty/service policy, privacy notice, tracking controls, restricted-product policy, tax-relief evidence flow and complaint handling. Display applicable terms before purchase and keep version/acceptance records. Align every document with the actual operating model; do not copy competitor licence statements or exclusions.
