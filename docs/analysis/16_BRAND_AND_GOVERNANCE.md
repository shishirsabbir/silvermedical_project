## Naming and identity

Choose a name that is pronounceable, distinctive and usable across the intended countries, with no implied regulator, NHS or manufacturer affiliation. Screen domains, company names, search results and relevant UK/EU trademark categories; company-name or domain availability alone is not trademark clearance. UK registration has territorial limits. [UK trademark search](https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark), [UK registration](https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark).

For shortlisted names, obtain EU/national trademark checks, relevant-language reviews and professional clearance before investing in packaging or publication. No candidate search or clearance is claimed in this baseline.

Display the correct seller identity, registered address, operational contact channels and fulfilment information consistently. Do not reuse Economedical Ltd's VAT number, DUNS, reviews or historical supplier claims unless the legal/business relationship is confirmed and use is justified.

## Team and responsibilities

| Function | Required ownership |
|---|---|
| Founder/governance | Entity, funding, contracts, strategy and accepted risk |
| Compliance/quality | Product routes, evidence, permissions, complaints and recalls |
| Licensed professional roles | Pharmacist/responsible person/other qualified staff where the activity requires them |
| Category/procurement | Range, supplier terms, authenticity, margin and continuity |
| Operations | Premises/3PL, inventory, storage, delivery and traceability |
| Finance/tax | VAT/customs, cash, credit, reconciliations and reporting |
| Product/data | Product information, access rules, integrations and audit trail |
| Support/service | Order ownership, technical escalation, warranty and aftercare |
| Marketing/privacy | Evidence-backed offers, lawful acquisition, retention and measurement |

One person may cover multiple functions where appropriate, but required professional appointments and competence must be established for actual activities. Avoid relying on a platform vendor or 3PL to own responsibilities that belong to the seller.

## Insurance and continuity

Obtain cover matched to human/veterinary goods, medicines if included, import/private-label exposure, territory, product liability, recall costs, stock, transit, service/installation, public liability and cyber risks. Employers' liability requirements depend on the employment situation and exemptions. Product liability is distinct from public or employers' cover. [Government product safety guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/product-safety-advice-for-businesses), [Business insurance guidance](https://www.business.gov.uk/support/regulations-and-licensing/insuring-your-business/).

Check exclusions for regulated goods, clinical advice, US/other territories, supplier-direct deliveries, temperature excursions and refurbished equipment. Keep cover, supplier indemnities and actual business activities aligned.

## Environmental responsibilities

Check UK packaging EPR using current producer/activity, group turnover and packaging-volume tests; keep packaging weights, material and ownership data from the outset. Importing, own-brand packing and distribution can create different responsibilities. Do not assume outsourcing fulfilment removes the seller's duties. [Current EPR applicability](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-you-must-comply-with-epr-for-packaging--2).

Check WEEE duties as retailer/distributor and separately as producer/importer where relevant. Current government guidance describes the Distributor Take Back Scheme for eligible sellers, with the referenced scheme period ending 31 December 2026; recheck the next period before relying on it. [DTS guidance](https://www.gov.uk/electricalwaste-producer-supplier-responsibilities/join-the-distributor-takeback-scheme).

Portable battery retail/distribution takeback is triggered at 32kg or more per year under current guidance. Embedded batteries, producer roles and other battery types need separate assessment. [Battery responsibilities](https://www.gov.uk/battery-waste-supplier-reponsibilities).

Selling empty clinical-waste containers is not the same business as collecting used clinical waste. If collection/disposal or contaminated returns are offered, establish the separate waste handling, carrier, contractor and documentation requirements before activation.

## Governance cadence

Weekly: cash, supplier exceptions, stock/expiry, delivery, complaints and blocked SKUs. Monthly: category contribution, cohort retention, aged receivables, evidence expiry and supplier concentration. Before each category/country release: review permissions, evidence, insurance, terms and operations. Log changed requirements and accepted decisions rather than silently overwriting the historical baseline.
