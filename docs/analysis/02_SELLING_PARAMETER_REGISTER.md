# Selling parameter register

Baseline: 7 November 2026. This is the master analysis checklist. Proposed controls and metrics below are design requirements, not claims that they are already implemented or universally mandated.

| Parameter | Decision or evidence required | Commercial effect | Working measure | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legal operator | Entity, establishment, registrations | Defines contracting party and responsibilities | Identity verified | 16 |
| Brand | Name, domain and trademark clearance | Trust and defensibility | Clearance completed | 16 |
| Geography | GB, NI and destination permissions | Sale eligibility and delivery cost | Approved destination coverage | 06, 14 |
| Buyer type | Consumer, practitioner, corporate, institution | Terms, VAT and checkout | Verified eligible accounts | 03, 10 |
| Buyer authority | Organisation, role, purchase authority, prescription where relevant | Prevents invalid restricted supply | Review turnaround | 05, 06 |
| Category scope | Full taxonomy and activation sequence | Breadth versus readiness | Approved revenue-weighted coverage | 05 |
| Product classification | Intended use and applicable regimes | Determines lawful supply route | Dossier completeness | 06 |
| Seller role | Distributor, importer, manufacturer, UKRP, pack producer | Cost and liability | Role assigned per lane/SKU | 06, 07 |
| Supplier legitimacy | Manufacturer/wholesaler authorisation and bank verification | Authenticity and continuity | Approved suppliers | 07 |
| Resale rights | Territory, online channel, images and trademarks | Avoids unsellable stock/content | Permission coverage | 07 |
| Product documentation | Declaration, certificates when applicable, IFU and labels | Approval and buyer confidence | Missing/expired records | 06, 11 |
| Claims | Intended purpose and substantiation | Classification and advertising exposure | Reviewed claims coverage | 06, 12 |
| Product identifiers | MPN, GTIN, UDI when applicable, variants | Accurate search and fulfilment | Identifier error rate | 11 |
| Units and packs | Unit/box/case, quantity, dimensions | Prevents wrong comparison/orders | Pack-related returns | 08, 11 |
| Compatibility | Consumables, accessories, model and revision | Basket value and fewer returns | Compatibility complaints | 11 |
| Origin/customs | Commodity code, origin, valuation and importer | Landed cost and clearance | Customs error/delay | 07 |
| Purchase cost | Discounts, rebates, minimum orders and FX | Margin and stock budget | Landed cost per sellable unit | 08 |
| VAT | Product/customer/supply eligibility, evidence | Correct gross price and tax | Tax exceptions and corrections | 08 |
| Price architecture | Gross/net display, unit price, promotions | Conversion and transparency | Basket price index | 08, 10 |
| Gross margin | Revenue less landed product cost | Basic category viability | Gross margin % by category | 08 |
| Contribution | All variable order costs | Growth economics | CM before/after acquisition | 08 |
| Delivery policy | Zones, cutoffs, bulky/hazardous/cold routes | Conversion and complaints | Delivered promise accuracy | 09 |
| Free shipping | Threshold versus incremental margin | AOV and margin leakage | Subsidy per order | 08 |
| Inventory | Stock ownership, allocation and buffers | Availability and cash | Fill rate/stock days | 09 |
| Expiry | Lot-level dates and minimum shelf life | Waste and customer acceptance | Writeoff and short-date rate | 09 |
| Storage | Manufacturer limits, monitoring and segregation | Product integrity | Excursions/quarantine | 09 |
| Traceability | Lots/serials to supplier receipts and customer orders | Recall scope and resolution | Trace drill completion | 09 |
| Returns | Consumer cancellation, B2B agreement, defects | Cost and trust | Refund time/reason rate | 10 |
| Warranty/service | Installation, repair, calibration, spares | Equipment viability | Resolution and service margin | 09 |
| Payments | PSP acceptance, settlement, fraud, refunds | Cash and conversion | Approval/chargeback rate | 08, 11 |
| Credit | Limits, verification, terms and collections | B2B growth and bad debt | DSO/overdue balance | 10 |
| Search/navigation | Specialties, identifiers and technical filters | Findability | Search success/zero results | 11 |
| Basket/account tools | Saved lists, repeat order, PO, multi-site | Retention and admin savings | Repeat order adoption | 11 |
| Support | Product expertise, order ownership and escalation | Trust and complaint cost | Response/resolution time | 09 |
| Acquisition | SEO, search ads, direct sales, partnerships | New customers | Fully loaded CAC | 12 |
| Retention | Replenishment, stock continuity, service | Cohort contribution | Repeat and retained revenue | 12 |
| Messaging channels | Email, SMS and WhatsApp use-case feasibility; provider acceptance | Reach, repeat purchasing and platform restrictions | Eligible reachable audience | 21 |
| Marketing permissions | Sender/channel/purpose basis, legacy data and suppression | Determines who may receive which campaigns | Permission evidence and suppression-sync coverage | 21 |
| Delivery and automation | Service templates, campaign journeys, authenticated senders, retries and stops | Customer trust and avoidable support demand | Failures, complaints and duplicate sends | 21 |
| CRM and lifecycle | Support history, segmentation, reordering and inactivity rules | Retention and service efficiency | Time to second order and resolution time | 21, 22 |
| Loyalty/referrals | Funding, incentives, redemption/refunds and contact permissions | Growth versus margin leakage | Incremental contribution after rewards | 22 |
| Handover and ongoing operation | Owners, training, support, service fees and change control | Maintainability and total cost | Accepted deliverables and staffed processes | 22 |
| Data/privacy | Lawful basis, consent, processors and retention | Customer access and compliance | Consent/DSAR/security events | 12 |
| Public procurement | Tender route and required evidence | Institutional access | Qualified bid pipeline | 13 |
| Sustainability | Packaging, WEEE, batteries, tender expectations | Fees and channel access | Obligations/packaging data | 13, 16 |
| Insurance | Product, recall, public, employers, cyber and stock | Residual risk financing | Cover/exclusions reviewed | 16 |
| Working capital | Inventory + receivables - payables | Funding requirement | Cash runway/cash cycle | 08 |
| EU localisation | Language, labels, VAT, roles and support | Destination conversion and supply eligibility | Country release status | 14 |

Detail numbers refer to the numbered files in this analysis directory. A product may be commercially attractive yet remain blocked because its evidence, fulfilment route or permitted buyer type is incomplete.
