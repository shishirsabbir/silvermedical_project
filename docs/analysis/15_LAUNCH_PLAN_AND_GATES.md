# Launch plan and gates

Baseline: 7 November 2026. Proposed sequence, not a fixed launch commitment. The full catalogue remains the ambition. Licence applications and complex equipment can take longer than the illustrative 90-day preparation cycle.

## Parallel workstreams

| Period | Work | Concrete output |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–15 | Operator, budget, buyer scope, geography, taxonomy, category role map | Accepted business brief and priority decisions |
| Days 1–30 | Buyer research, supplier qualification, matched basket | Interview synthesis, written quotes and cost comparison |
| Days 15–45 | Product dossiers, UK/EU import lanes, premises/3PL review | Approved candidate SKUs and fulfilment design |
| Days 20–60 | Tax/terms, platform evaluation, product data and support workflows | Requirements, provider quotes and reviewed policy requirements |
| Days 45–75 | Pilot setup, traceability, refund, delivery and failure exercises | Evidence from end-to-end operational trials |
| Days 60–90 | Limited release of ready categories and measured acquisition | Paid-pilot contribution, service and cash results |
| Independent timelines | Medicines, controlled/cold-chain products, complex IVDs, installations | Activity-specific authorisations, staff and verified capability |
| Later country cycles | First EU destination and fulfilment choice | Country-specific release dossier |

The day ranges are planning assumptions, not evidence of regulator approval times or a promise of full-range launch within 90 days.

## Release gates

| Gate | Evidence required | Proposed accountable role |
|---|---|---|
| G1 Business identity | Legal seller, bank, tax, brand and addresses consistent | Founder/finance |
| G2 Activity permissions | Category, role, destination and licence route documented | Compliance lead and specialist reviewer |
| G3 Product approval | Authentic supplier, valid product dossier, claims and buyer eligibility | Category/compliance |
| G4 Commercial viability | Quote-based landed costs, priced basket, contribution and cash budget | Finance/category |
| G5 Fulfilment | Stock truth, lots/serials, expiry, storage and carrier acceptance | Operations/3PL |
| G6 Customer journeys | Accurate tax/total, eligibility, acceptance, dispatch, returns/refunds | Product/operations |
| G7 Service and safety | Complaint escalation, warranty, mock recall and continuity | Quality/support |
| G8 Growth readiness | Measurement, message permissions, tested offers and cohort economics | Marketing/finance |
| G9 New country | Complete country dossier and tested order/return | Expansion lead |

A gate can be passed for one category and blocked for another. Do not invent a single whole-store approval badge.

## Risk register

| Risk | Effect | Control or evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong product route or operator role | Unsellable stock and liability | Per-SKU/lane classification and specialist review |
| Invalid evidence/counterfeit supply | Patient harm and recall | Supplier verification, document/model matching and traceability |
| Imported stock without GB/NI eligibility | Border or market failure | Route approval before purchase |
| Supplier stock overstated | Delivery complaints and refunds | Fresh feeds, acceptance rules and honest promises |
| Thin margin after freight/discounts | Growth burns cash | CM1/CM2 by basket and shipping class |
| Customer credit versus upfront stock cost | Funding shortfall | Limits, collections and 13-week cash forecast |
| Short-dated/slow inventory | Writeoff and reputational damage | FEFO, minimum shelf life and aged-stock review |
| Missing lot-to-order link | Ineffective recall | Timed trace drill including supplier-direct orders |
| Inadequate equipment service | Warranty losses and downtime | Manufacturer service contract and costed SLA |
| Medicines handled as ordinary goods | Licence/storage/supply breaches | Separate authorised workstream and professional controls |
| Overbroad claims/tracking/marketing | Enforcement and trust loss | Content review, consent rules and suppression |
| EU expansion by shipping toggle alone | Tax, conformity and return failures | Country/lane release gate |

## Evidence needed next

Confirm the operator, budget, buyer policy and initial activation order; obtain at least comparable quotations across UK and EU candidates; fill the matched basket; validate demand with interviews; and get proposals from fulfilment providers capable of the actual product mix. These are substantive next steps, not approvals requested by this document.

Until those inputs exist, financial forecasts, launch dates and product availability remain unverified. Keep open gaps in memory/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md and accepted choices in memory/DECISIONS.md.
