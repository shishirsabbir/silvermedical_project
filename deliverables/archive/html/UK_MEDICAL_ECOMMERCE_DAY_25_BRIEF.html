Medical commerce / UK launch8 November 2026

UK medical ecommerce

Project scope, delivery and launch budget

Phase 1 target: start selling on Day 25

A new brand buying and holding medical stock from UK/EU suppliers, selling to consumers and verified professionals. UK first; Europe later. Praxisdienst is the benchmark. Activate each product after review; the full catalogue remains the ambition.

25 daysPhase 1 target
~£7.7kIllustrative launch budget
~£85/moTechnology allowance

01What we will develop first

Storefront: mobile Next.js website, categories/search, accurate product and pack details, consumer prices, account/guest checkout and order history.

Admin: React product/import review, upload-time image optimisation to R2, supplier records, owned stock, lot/expiry tracking where needed, orders, dispatch, refunds, staff roles and audit history.

Hosting/commerce: Next.js, admin, API and PostgreSQL on Hostinger KVM 4; Cloudflare domain/R2. Revolut with Apple Pay/Google Pay and PayPal, subject to approval; VAT/delivery rules, professional checks, reservations and order emails. API recommendation: NestJS/Fastify, still open.

02How we will sell lawfully

Approve suppliers and each SKU before publication: intended use, evidence, content rights, eligible buyer, destination, storage and traceability. Receive stock, verify it, then offer only available, approved items.

Product routes: devices/IVDs need the applicable MHRA/conformity route; PPE, biocides and medicines have separate rules. Assess GB and Northern Ireland separately. Prescription medicines remain undecided and gated pending the authorised supply route. [1]

Customer rules: clear seller identity and total prices; VAT by transaction; lawful cancellation, returns and faulty-goods rights. Apply UK GDPR/PECR, secure records and channel-specific messaging permissions. [2]

Promotion: reviewed SEO and Google feeds only. UK prescription-drug listings are excluded from Merchant Center; the UK OTC route requires pharmacy registration/certification. WhatsApp has healthcare-product restrictions and needs a separate feasibility review. [3,6]

03Ideas for earlier traction

Start with a curated, compliant, in-stock assortment; test a matched-price basket and clinic starter packs. Offer fast quote turnaround, clear pack prices, reliable dispatch and reorder lists. Seek manufacturer referrals and pilot clinic customers. Add permission-based welcome, stock-alert and replenishment emails; measure repeat contribution, not traffic alone.

04The 25-day implementation

Days 1-3: freeze launch scope/SKUs, API and reuse choices; establish seller, supplier evidence, tax/delivery rules, domain/KVM and payment applications.

Days 4-10: database/API, React admin, stock/import review, R2 media and Next.js shopping pages. Reuse proven components; review and test AI-assisted work.

Days 11-17: checkout, approved payments/webhooks, stock reservations, VAT, dispatch/refunds, service emails, preferences and professional order checks.

Days 18-24: approve launch data/policies; test complete orders, failed/duplicate payments, refunds, fulfilment, permissions, mobile/accessibility, peak load and backup restoration.

Day 25: controlled release only with lawful stock, accepted payments, ready fulfilment/support and passing order/refund, access and restore checks. Requires daily owner decisions; AI agents cannot guarantee the date.

05Budget and recurring costs

Planning example, not a quotation: 200 developer hours × £30 = £6,000; 20% reserve = £1,200; AI/tools allowance £150; initial technology cash ~£318. Total ~£7,668. Replace the rate and hours with the developer's agreed quote.

Technology: base ~£70/month; allow £85/month initially or £105/month at VPS renewal. Includes modelled email/marketing, one mailbox, backups, monitoring, a domain and 100GB R2. KVM 4 offer: £239.76 prepaid for 24 months; £24.99/month equivalent at renewal. [4]

Separate costs: payment fees, VAT, stock, freight, premises, licences/professional review, insurance, staff, maintenance, advertising and campaign work. R2 overage: $0.015/GB-month above the 10GB Standard allowance, plus request overages. [5]

06Later phases and exclusions

Later: wider category/country activation, advanced B2B credit/approvals, ERP/carrier connectors, richer CRM/automation, loyalty/referrals and equipment service workflows. SMS/WhatsApp and medicines workflows require separate decisions.

Outside the current build: multi-vendor marketplace, dropshipping, native mobile apps, automated clinical prescribing, a custom email platform and copying competitor content without rights. Stripe is excluded.

Day 1 starts on the agreed project date. Day 25 is a conditional release target, not completion of every future feature. Business name, legal operator, final budget and medicines route remain open.
