## Planning implications

Keep legal identity, fulfilment promises and support contacts consistent; establish actual inventory and traceability before making claims; own delivery exceptions and refunds; validate supplier/cash economics; and avoid copying competitors' terms without determining the new venture's actual buyer model and duties. These are recommendations drawn from the research, not statements that the new venture has inherited any earlier business's problems or assets.
