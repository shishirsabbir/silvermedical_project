# Public sector and NHS sales

Baseline: 7 November 2026. No NHS contract, supplier approval or framework position is established for the new business.

## Different routes to purchasing

Distinguish individual practice purchases, local organisation procurement, framework call-offs, competitive tenders and NHS Supply Chain arrangements. A website that accepts an NHS email address is not thereby an NHS-approved supplier. NHS England describes multiple purchasing routes and Atamis use across much of the health family. [Supplying to the NHS](https://www.england.nhs.uk/nhs-commercial/supplying-to-the-nhs/).

Find a Tender is the central digital platform under the Procurement Act framework, with supplier registration and stored core information. Monitor relevant notices and frameworks, then check each opportunity's geography and rules. Scotland and other devolved procurement routes require their own treatment; do not call one portal a complete UK procurement map. [Central digital platform guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-procure-phase/guidance-central-digital-platform-and-publication-of-information-html).

## Supplier evidence pack

Prepare company/ownership details, verified bank details, tax/financial information, insurance, product conformity evidence, supplier authorisations, quality procedures, recall and business-continuity plans, traceability, references and delivery capability. Add GS1/other catalogue identifiers, e-invoicing and procurement-system requirements where the buyer requests them.

Tender questions may cover social value, modern slavery, environmental performance, cybersecurity, subcontractors, equality and workforce practices. Requirements depend on the buyer, contract and value; do not describe a quality certification or cyber badge as universal for all NHS sales.

NHS England's Evergreen tool supports supplier sustainability assessment; it is not a product approval. Carbon-reduction and net-zero expectations must be matched to the procurement and current NHS roadmap. [Evergreen](https://www.england.nhs.uk/nhs-commercial/sustainability/evergreen/), [NHS Supply Chain sustainability](https://www.supplychain.nhs.uk/programmes/sustainability/).

## Contract economics

Model bid preparation, sampling, evaluation, catalogue loading, customer-specific packing, reports, invoicing, delivery frequency, price caps, rebates, service credits, stock commitments and collection time. Contract revenue can be large while contribution and cash are weak.

Verify PO legitimacy and invoicing destination. Define account limits and escalation rather than granting unlimited credit based on a public-body name. Keep patient information out of commercial order records.

## Proposed sequence

Establish reliable ordinary professional sales and evidence before bidding for service levels that the operation cannot yet meet. This is a readiness recommendation, not a restriction on the full-product plan. Maintain a pipeline containing notice URL, contracting body, dates, category, qualification gaps, bid cost, forecast contribution and decision owner.
